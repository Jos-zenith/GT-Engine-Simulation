06 Genetic-algorithm optimisation & validation

ML SEARCH → PHYSICS CHECK

Differential evolution searches the continuous input space on the ML surrogate using the weights and knock limit from section 02. The optimum is then re-simulated with the physics model: the gap between the two is the surrogate's real error at that point.

H₂ ENGINE STUDIO Results are simulated with literature-typical constants, not measured; validate against GT-Power or test data before quantitative claims.
